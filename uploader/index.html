Cycles One-Shot Upload

Puts audio files on the +Drive of a Model:Cycles over USB, as samples SAMPLE can play. Use Chrome or Edge; the page asks for MIDI access with SysEx.

1. Connect

Connect the Cycles by USB and close Elektron Transfer, which would share the port.

2. Folder on the Cycles

/

    × deletes a sample, or a folder with everything in it, from the Cycles after asking.

    3. Files

      Each file becomes a 48 kHz mono sample named as listed. The Cycles stays silent while it writes.
